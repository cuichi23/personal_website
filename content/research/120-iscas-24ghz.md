The step from a benchtop demonstration to microwave frequency. Two phase-locked loops with
24 GHz voltage-controlled oscillators were coupled bidirectionally, using a PLL architecture
built for mutual rather than hierarchical coupling. The existing phase-domain model was
extended to include the oscillator's nonlinear response to the tuning signal, after which
the frequencies and phase relations of the self-organized states are predicted precisely
rather than approximately. Measurements at several delays and division factors agree, and
for delays up to 14 ns self-organized synchronization is shown to be feasible at microwave
frequencies.
