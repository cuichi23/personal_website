Oscillators with an inert response are the normal case, from biology to engineering, and
they admit synchronized states whose frequency is not constant. Such states show up as side
bands in the spectrum or as chaotic dynamics. Their stability had only been accessible
numerically, because the delay makes the problem multistable. We derive criteria and
conditions that decide linear stability analytically, directly from the system parameters,
for arbitrary network topologies with identical oscillators and identical delays. It is the
criterion the later work in this list leans on, and the reason a claim of stability there
can be checked rather than simulated.
