The companion to the ICC paper of the same year, presented at a workshop on biological and
multi-scale communication. It makes the analogy explicit that the rest of this work usually
leaves implicit: the delay-coupled oscillator mechanism that keeps cells in a developing
embryo in step is the same mechanism that can clock an antenna array, and the architecture
it suggests is flat and reference-free.
