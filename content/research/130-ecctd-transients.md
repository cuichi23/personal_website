Classical phase-locked loop theory assumes a single input and no delay. This paper
generalizes it to include time delays and mutual coupling, for loops of arbitrary order and
arbitrary number of inputs, and gives two methods for finding locked states and their
transient dynamics. For entrainment the generalized and the classical theory overlap, which
is the consistency check. Measurements on a classical PLL entrained by a clock confirm the
predicted phase relations, the dependence of the loop gain on component characteristics and
delay, and both the decay rate and the decay frequency of a perturbation.
