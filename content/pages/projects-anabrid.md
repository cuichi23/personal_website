These are problems we are putting on a physical computer rather than stepping
through one. The REDAC solves a differential equation by being a system that
obeys it, so the question is never only whether the answer is right. It is also
what the machine does to the physics on the way, and what has to be done to the
machine to get the physics back.

Every project below is work in progress. The public description says what is
being investigated and why that is hard on this kind of machine. What is
measured, what failed, and what we do about it is behind the password on each
page, because most of it is unpublished and some of it belongs to partners.
This section is separate from the [oscillatory computing](/oscillatory-computing/)
work, which is my own research and carries its own password.
