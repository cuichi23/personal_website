A network of coupled oscillators does not only keep time. It settles, and which state it
settles into is fixed by the conditions it was placed in rather than by an instruction.
Read the right way, a state a network settles into is an answer to something. That is the
direction this page is about, and the reason the
[synchronization work](/research/) and the analog computing sit in the same career.

The panel behind the glass belongs to a manuscript that is still in review. Its model, its
parameters and what it computes are behind the password with it, and stay there until the
manuscript is out. If you want to look at it before then, ask me.
