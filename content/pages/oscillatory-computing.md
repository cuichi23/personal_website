A network of coupled oscillators does not only keep time. It settles, and which state it
settles into is fixed by the conditions it was placed in rather than by an instruction.
Read the right way, a state a network settles into is an answer to something. That is the
direction these projects are about, and the reason the [synchronization work](/research/)
and the analog computing sit in the same career.

The projects below share one password, separate from the anabrid work. Each page says in
the open what the work is and why it is hard. The model, the interactive bench and the
numbers are behind the password, because most of this is unpublished. If you want to look
before it is out, ask me.
