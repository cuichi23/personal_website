Everything else here is written to be read. This page is not. Below are two
applets I built for my own children, each one a thing to take hold of rather
than a thing to work through: a blind map of Europe where the names have to go
back where they belong, and an optics lab where light travels in straight
lines until something gets in the way.

Both are free of accounts, timers, scores and badges. Both keep their progress
in your own browser and send nothing anywhere. Both carry a language switch in
their top right corner — **DE · EN · ES · FR** — and nothing on the page needs
a grown-up standing by, which was the point.

**Auf Deutsch:** such dir oben rechts in jedem Kasten deine Sprache aus und
fang einfach an. Was du richtig hast, bleibt richtig, auch wenn du die Seite
morgen wieder aufmachst. · **En español:** elige tu idioma arriba a la derecha
de cada recuadro. · **En français :** choisis ta langue en haut à droite de
chaque encadré.

## Stumme Karte — the blind map of Europe {: #stumme-karte }

170 names, five at a time, dragged onto a map that carries every coastline,
river, mountain ridge and capital dot and not one label. A name that lands
correctly stays on the map, so the map fills up while you play. Countries,
capitals, seas, rivers, mountain ranges, islands and landmarks, at three
levels of difficulty, with zoom and full screen for the small countries.

## Strahlenoptik — the optics lab {: #strahlenoptik }

Five stations of ray optics: light and shadow, the law of reflection,
refraction and total reflection, the focal point of a lens, and an optical
bench with a lamp, an object, a lens on a rail and a projection screen.
Eighteen tasks that check themselves, so nobody has to mark them.
