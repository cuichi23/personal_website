Current projects

Playground

The things on this site that are meant to be played with rather than read. A blind map of Europe and a small optics lab, both built for my own children, both in German, English, Spanish and French.

Everything else here is written to be read. This page is not. Below are two applets I built for my own children, each one a thing to take hold of rather than a thing to work through: a blind map of Europe where the names have to go back where they belong, and an optics lab where light travels in straight lines until something gets in the way.

Both are free of accounts, timers, scores and badges. Both keep their progress in your own browser and send nothing anywhere. Both carry a language switch in their top right corner — DE · EN · ES · FR — and nothing on the page needs a grown-up standing by, which was the point.

Auf Deutsch: such dir oben rechts in jedem Kasten deine Sprache aus und fang einfach an. Was du richtig hast, bleibt richtig, auch wenn du die Seite morgen wieder aufmachst. · En español: elige tu idioma arriba a la derecha de cada recuadro. · En français : choisis ta langue en haut à droite de chaque encadré.

Stumme Karte — the blind map of Europe

170 names, five at a time, dragged onto a map that carries every coastline, river, mountain ridge and capital dot and not one label. A name that lands correctly stays on the map, so the map fills up while you play. Countries, capitals, seas, rivers, mountain ranges, islands and landmarks, at three levels of difficulty, with zoom and full screen for the small countries.

How it was built, what the projection is and where the map can be wrong: Stumme Karte: a blind map of Europe.

Strahlenoptik — the optics lab

Five stations of ray optics: light and shadow, the law of reflection, refraction and total reflection, the focal point of a lens, and an optical bench with a lamp, an object, a lens on a rail and a projection screen. Eighteen tasks that check themselves, so nobody has to mark them.

What it models, what it leaves out and how it is checked: Strahlenoptik: a small optics lab.
