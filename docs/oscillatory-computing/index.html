Oscillatory computing

My own research on computing with networks of coupled oscillators. Each project is described in the open; the models, the live benches and the measurements sit behind a password.

A network of coupled oscillators does not only keep time. It settles, and which state it settles into is fixed by the conditions it was placed in rather than by an instruction. Read the right way, a state a network settles into is an answer to something. That is the direction these projects are about, and the reason the synchronization work and the analog computing sit in the same career.

The projects below share one password, separate from the anabrid work. Each page says in the open what the work is and why it is hard. The model, the interactive bench and the numbers are behind the password, because most of this is unpublished. If you want to look before it is out, ask me.

  1. in review

    PLL-based oscillatory networks

    A square lattice of delay-coupled phase oscillators with a second, order-parameter channel, run live in the browser. The model and what it computes sit behind the password until the manuscript is out.

  2. demonstrators

    GNSS-independent localization and timing

    Mutually coupled clocks that hold true time without GNSS, detect a spoofing carry-off as a divergence from their own consensus, and hear the direction of a signal well enough to place a receiver or a hostile emitter. Two interactive benches behind the password.
